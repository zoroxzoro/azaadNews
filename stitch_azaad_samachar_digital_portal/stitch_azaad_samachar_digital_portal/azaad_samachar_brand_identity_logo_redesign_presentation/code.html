Brand Architecture Audit: Before & After

Key visual and typographic improvements resolving previous production issues.

Previous Logo Drawbacks ✖ Outdated
  • Heavy outer blue and red circular rings occupied excessive horizontal space in modern web navigation bars.
  • Outdated drop shadows, neon wave arcs, and bevel gradients created visual clutter at small digital sizes.
  • Devanagari font used generic strokes with competing dual colors that reduced instant readability.
  • Tagline was cramped under the wordmark inside the dark badge, making it illegible on mobile devices.
Redesigned Identity Benefits ✔ Production Ready
  • Preserves 100% of the recognized, authentic eagle mascot without alteration, maintaining trusted brand equity.
  • Introduces high-contrast Devanagari typography with authoritative primary red (#C8102E) and institutional navy (#10264B).
  • Provides a versatile 3-lockup system (Horizontal for mastheads, Stacked for promos, Emblem for app icons/favicons).
  • Clean transparent background eliminates bulky borders, allowing seamless integration into any light or dark portal layout.
